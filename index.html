Martin Property Consultants are delighted to present Flat 15, Blackhall Court, Dublin 7, to the market. This well-located two-bedroom apartment offers an excellent opportunity for first-time buyers, owner-occupiers, or investors seeking a property in a highly convenient Dublin 7 location.

The accommodation comprises a welcoming entrance hall, a bright living and dining area, a separate kitchen, two well-proportioned bedrooms and a main bathroom. The apartment provides well-balanced accommodation and offers scope for a new owner to modernise and personalise the space to their own taste.

Accommodation

Blackhall Court is ideally positioned on Blackhall Place, within easy reach of Smithfield, Stoneybatter and Dublin city centre. The surrounding area offers an excellent selection of cafés, restaurants, bars, shops and everyday amenities, while the nearby Luas Red Line provides convenient access throughout the city and beyond.

The apartment is also well positioned for access to TU Dublin Grangegorman, the Four Courts, the Law Library and a number of major employment and educational centres. Dublin city centre is within easy walking distance, making this an attractive location for both owner-occupiers and tenants.

The property would make an excellent city-centre home or investment and benefits from its established location close to a wide range of amenities and transport links.
